# DISCOVERY §66: model-based arms on the expensive track (in-sample tables)

The TR rows (`RR_TRQ*`, `Blocks3_TRQ`, rules) are the arm after the #383 review fixes (rank test, least-change Hessian, one shrink per model state).  The pre-fix TR rows (commit 1301d2e) are kept at the end for the record.

**In sample.**  Seeds 3/7/42/1234/2025 (the first 5 of the roster), the
same seeds, instances and CRN durations as §62/§65.  Local runs on the
`scripts/measure.py` core path (`run_family_harness`, free preset, 3
instances per family, virtual clock: async, log-normal σ 0.5, CRN per
cell; `sync_eval=True`).  Blocks and the pool are the runner units of §65
(core: run 36313485264; qLogEI / TuRBO1: run 36274781342); a local
re-run of Blocks on d2/100·d/q1/s3 is bit-identical to the runner unit.

Δ = spec − reference, paired over the 5 seeds on the common (seed,
instance) runs, t-CI95 over seeds, wins/5, unadjusted.  "pool best" is
§65's pool best of the cell (fixed by the full-cell mean).  Headline metric:
AOCC at q = 1, `aocc_time` at q = 4.

Specs: `RR_COBYQA` / `RR_TRQ` = the arm alone under `StrategyRoundRobin`;
`Blocks3_X` = `Blocks_warm_CMAES_JSO` with X as a third arm (seed_name of
Blocks); `RR_TRQ[first_start="random"]` = the random-start ablation;
`Rule_dim` = RR_TRQ at d = 2, Blocks3_TRQ at d ≥ 5; `Rule_probe` = RR_TRQ at
d = 2, Blocks3_TRQ at d = 5, at d = 10 Blocks3_TRQ on the ellipsoid
instances and Blocks elsewhere (what an f-scale quadratic-R² probe would
pick; the probe's evaluations are not charged, so it is an oracle upper bound).  Both rules were chosen
after seeing these numbers.

`RR_COBYQA` is seed-invariant (box-centre start, no randomness): its five
"seeds" are one run, so its CIs carry only the reference's variance.
`Blocks3_NelderMead` ran at q = 1 only; `RR_NelderMead` and
`RR_QuadWLS` produce nothing alone (they need a Splitter best box from
another arm) and are not listed.
